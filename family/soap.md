The Soap Theory Returns

A few years after the great flower shampoo incident, Emily had just turned seven.

We were talking on the phone, catching up on things, when out of the blue she asked me a very serious question: 

why do people go blind?

I told her that it just happens to some people for different reasons.

There was a quiet pause on the line while she considered this heavy medical reality.

After a few seconds, she let out a knowing sigh and said, "It was soap, wasn't it?"

I couldn't help it—I just laughed and got to tell her the flower shampoo story all over again.
