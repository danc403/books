The Driver in Reverse

When my oldest granddaughter, Elizabeth, was about five or six, she would often ride along with my wife to pick me up from my truck when I got home for the weekend. One of the first times they arrived early enough to watch me maneuver, I was parked at a tight little Dollar General a few miles from our house. That parking lot was a nightmare—you had to pull in front of the store between cars and then back around the side, weaving through a very narrow gap to reach the back of the lot. They were on the phone with me as I pulled in, and I told them to hold on for a second while I made the move. As I started backing around the side, I heard Elizabeth screaming in the background: "He's going backwards! He's driving backwards!" She couldn't wrap her head around how someone could pilot something that large in reverse, but the sheer shock in her voice was the best welcome home I could have asked for.
