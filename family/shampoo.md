The Flower Shampoo Theory

Shortly after I went blind, my granddaughter Emily—who was about four or five at the time—approached me with a story that was deeply traumatic for her. She told me about a time her mom was giving her a bath and she accidentally got "flower shampoo" in her eyes. She told me, "I went blind just like Poppy, but then I could see again." She looked at me with genuine concern and asked, "Did you get flower shampoo in your eyes, Poppy?" She was absolutely convinced that flower shampoo was the sole cause of blindness, and she was relieved to have dodged a bullet herself. I still laugh every time I think about it; in a child's world, the most complex medical realities are always explained by the simplest of household accidents.
