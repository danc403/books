The Night-Owl Pipeline

When I was driving a semi, my oldest grandson, Kyler, had a habit of sneaking his mom’s phone into his bedroom at bedtime. When I was in town, I’d stop by to let him sit in the big truck, which was the highlight of his week. He figured out that if he called me, he could get a direct line to Poppy. I’d be on the road, see my daughter’s name pop up on the screen, and answer to a quiet, urgent whisper: "Poppy? You awake?" He would spend those late nights telling me everything about his day, building a connection that bypassed the house rules. Eventually, we’d hear the muffled sound of his mom opening his door, asking, "What are you doing under that blanket?"—and Kyler would get caught, mid-conversation with his co-conspirator.
