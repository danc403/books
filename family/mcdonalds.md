The Old MacDonald Meal

Another time, my wife was on the phone with me while she was picking Elizabeth up from kindergarten. As soon as Elizabeth climbed into the car, she was clearly thinking about lunch. She looked at my wife and asked, "Mimi, are we going to Old MacDonald's?" It took us a second to realize what she meant—she was hungry, she wanted a Happy Meal, and in her five-year-old logic, the place with the golden arches and the toys was obviously "Old MacDonald's." She got exactly what she was asking for, and "Old MacDonald's" has been the family name for that drive-thru ever since.
