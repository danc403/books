We got my granddaughter Adaline a makeup set for her third birthday.

Mimi must have picked well, because Adaline carried that kit everywhere for a solid week.

She would play with it for hours every day, and we would tell her how beautiful she was no matter how she mangled it.

A couple of weeks in, she was in my room playing with her set, narrating what she was doing and asking if I liked this color or that.

Her: Do you like bwoo?

Me: Yes, blue is a wonderful color.

Her: Because you a boy?

Me: Well I guess, but girls like blue too.

This went on for every color in her box.

After an hour or so, she started dabbing at my face along with hers.

One for me, one for you, I guess. I played along, telling her she was a maestro and generally making over her.

Every time she dabbed me again, she would say, "Poppy, I not done yet..."

After several rounds of this, she exclaimed, "Oh Poppy, I make you so prittiful! But I not done yet..."

Apparently it was a bigger job than she thought to begin with!
