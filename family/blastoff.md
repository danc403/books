The Last Blast Off

I loved kids. Especially toddlers. Thinking back to my first baby, I did mine and his first "Blast off." It was pretty simple, I guess. I would just count down from 3 to 1, yell "Blast Off!", pick him up, and twist him around in the air a bit, occasionally "getting" his belly. The screams of laughter were addictive to me as much as they were to him.

I did this with all my kids, and it grew to include all the nieces and nephews and friends. At one point, there were seven kids lined up to blast off. It made for a long night of laughing little kids, and I was known for it in the extended family. I was proud of the fact that all the kids looked forward to coming to our house. It was a family joke for years after they all got too big for me to blast them off—a great memory.

Then the grandkids started coming, and Poppy got another round. I have enjoyed another 10 years of Blast Offs. Sitting tonight and thinking, after another round of "3, 2, Bwas Off" (as my youngest granddaughter calls it), I realize this is likely the last baby that I will have the pleasure of blasting off. It is bittersweet. Actually, just bitter, I guess—at least until she comes back in five minutes. And off we go again. Live for the moment!

The Poppy Playbook: A Glossary of Terms

"Bwas Off": Blast Off! The foundational ritual that started it all.

"Updown": A versatile move. In its simplest form, it is holding a child while standing up and sitting down repeatedly. In the extended version, it is mixed with other activities like Bwas Off and Iggy Ack, involving a general jostling motion in an up-and-down direction.

"Iggy Ack": Piggy Back, typically including a synchronized round of Updowns.

"Hand Sink": A collaborative session of washing hands in the sink, which is really just a cover for playing in the water.
