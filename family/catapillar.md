The Construction King

When Kyler was five, he called me one afternoon vibrating with excitement. He was talking so fast I could barely parse the words, just endless rambling about a "catapillar." He kept asking, "Can I have a catapillar?" I was in "Poppy mode," so I agreed to everything. I didn't realize until later that he wasn't talking about a bug—he was talking about an RC Caterpillar, a full-blown remote-controlled construction set. He got the set, and it was worth every penny to hear the pride in his voice when he was the king of the block and his friends came to play with him and those machines.
